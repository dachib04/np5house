Panel House · Single Floor · L-Shaped Plan

TheBox Slope

A single floor, a clear idea. The Box Slope is organised around one unbroken level — light entering through large windows, three bedrooms arranged around a 47 m² kitchen-living room. The sloped roofline rises to 5.38 metres, giving the house its silhouette without the cost or complexity of multiple floors.

An integrated carport, two bathrooms, and a cleanly resolved utility zone complete the plan. The L-shaped footprint creates a sheltered courtyard relationship between the house and its site.
