Panel House · Two Floors · Gable Roof · Garage

TheCupcake Pack

Two floors, a complete family home. The Cupcake Pack rises through a 51 m² open living floor below and a fully private upper floor above. The gable roof creates an architectural attic space lit by skylights — a second floor that feels entirely different from a conventional upper level.

Ground floor: kitchen-living, garage, workroom, bathroom, boiler room. Second floor: parents' bedroom, two children's rooms, a large closet, and a second bathroom. Every space is resolved independently.
