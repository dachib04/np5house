The case for prefab architecture

Traditional construction is slow, expensive, and exposed to weather, labour shortages, and site delays. NP5 panel homes are manufactured in a controlled factory environment — weatherproof from day one, structurally precise, and ready to ship.

From factory production start to a habitable residence in as little as 90 days. Faster construction means faster rental income, faster resale, and faster return on capital — without compromising on quality or architecture.
